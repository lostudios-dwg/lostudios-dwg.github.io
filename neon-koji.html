NeonKōji

AI Typography / SOD-620P-05 / Spring 2025

NeonKōji is a speculative typeface born from the illuminated scaffolding of a future Tokyo that never existed. Inspired by the layered chaos of cyberpunk cityscapes, this typographic project reimagines each letterform as a miniature storefront: self-contained machines that hum with synthetic life, vending data, dreams, and fragments of memory.
